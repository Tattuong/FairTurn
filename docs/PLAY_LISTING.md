## Title
FairTurn

## Short description
Fair turns for a group. Whose turn is it?

## Full description
FairTurn answers “Whose turn is it?”. It is not bill-splitting, and it is not a chore checklist.

Create a group for food runs, duty, driving, or meeting prep. The app picks the present person with the fewest completed turns. A tie goes to whoever has waited longest, and the reason is shown under the name.

Skip a turn, swap the person, and write a reason. Anyone marked away stays out of the current turn. History shows how many times each person was assigned.

Coins are spent in the shop. Remove ads and FairTurn Pro are one-time unlocks. There is no account.

Remote config: https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N299.json — when disable=1, the Play purchase sheet is hidden. The shop still spends coins.
